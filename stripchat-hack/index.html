    Change that password everywhere else you reused it Password reuse is how one leaked login becomes a full identity problem. Start with your email account — it is the master key to everything else.

    Report the scam site Report phishing to Google Safe Browsing and to your national CERT or consumer-protection authority. Reports get malicious domains blocklisted and protect the next person.
